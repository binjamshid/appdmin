# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Appdmin serves an individual software owner who wants one private operational surface for products,
store performance, inference spend, people, infrastructure, and controlled actions. They use it on
desktop for investigation and configuration and on mobile for quick health checks and deliberate
interventions.

## Product purpose

Appdmin is a self-hosted, adapter-driven operations console. It should reveal what needs attention,
explain where every fact came from, and make it safe to act without forcing the owner across a
collection of vendor consoles.

## Brand personality

Premium, calm, precise, and technically candid. The visual character borrows Apple's restraint and
craft while retaining the density of a serious operations tool.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative gradients, glassmorphism, generic SaaS card walls, loud enterprise templates,
playful consumer styling, and sparse minimalism that hides evidence. Craft comes from typography,
alignment, interaction quality, truthful states, and disciplined materials.

## Design principles

1. Surface attention before totals.
2. Distinguish zero, unknown, unavailable, stale, delayed, and no source.
3. Keep definitions, provenance, and freshness close to every important value.
4. Reveal detail progressively without sending the owner to another screen unnecessarily.
5. Treat mobile and desktop as different operational contexts, not scaled copies.
6. Show only capabilities that installed adapters can actually provide.

## Accessibility

Appdmin has no formal conformance target for version one, but preserves practical essentials that
improve daily use: readable contrast, visible focus, adequate touch targets, reduced-motion support,
semantic forms, and status indicators that do not depend on color alone.
